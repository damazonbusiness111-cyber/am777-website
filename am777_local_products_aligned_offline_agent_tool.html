Commission Rules

Agent reference

Commission follows exactly what you mark as sold — add or remove an item and totals recalculate immediately.
Company expenses, margins, and profit are never shown — only your estimated commission.
If a sold item has no confirmed price (e.g. Complex Integration), it shows Pending and is excluded from totals until AM777 confirms the price.
Renewals are not guaranteed; any renewal commission applies only when the customer actually renews and pays.
Custom scope, discounts, multi-branch complexity, and integrations require AM777 confirmation before commitment.
